About Me

Instructional Designer & LMS Architect

PMP-certified instructional designer and LMS architect with 10+ years delivering end-to-end eLearning solutions for humanitarian NGOs, UN agencies, continental trade bodies, and universities across East Africa, Europe, and North America. I take projects from needs assessment and instructional design through multimedia production, LMS architecture, and post-launch evaluation.

I've led national LMS rollouts reaching 500,000+ students, built a self-learning course-quality intelligence plugin for Moodle, deployed Python ML backends for dropout prediction, co-developed AfCFTA trade eLearning modules with continental experts, and founded Instracta to deliver custom eLearning consulting at scale.

How I Design

From complexity to clarity - every time.

A structured design process that converts messy training requirements into learning experiences that stick and results you can measure.

Analyse

Needs assessment, stakeholder interviews, and performance gap analysis to define the real problem before a single slide is built.

Design

Instructional blueprint, learning objectives, and interaction strategy - mapped to ADDIE or SAM depending on the project timeline.

Develop

Authoring in Storyline, Rise, or Elucidat with SME review cycles built in. SCORM/xAPI packaging for measurable LMS delivery.

Deploy & Measure

LMS deployment, pilot testing, and a post-launch evaluation framework so you can see exactly what the learning achieved.
