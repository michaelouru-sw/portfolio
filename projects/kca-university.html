LMS & Media Services Transformation

KCA University · 2026 · Moodle / Articulate Rise

View Learning Management System Launch Sample Module

The Problem

KCA University's LMS operations were outdated and faculty had limited skills in producing digital, multimedia-rich course content — a performance gap that was limiting the university's ability to modernize its virtual campus experience for students.

Target Audience

University faculty with varying digital literacy who needed to become independent content creators, and the broader student body who would experience the modernized virtual campus.

My Role

Led the LMS overhaul and faculty capacity-building program as project manager, working with instructional designers, multimedia trainers, LMS administrators, and accessibility experts, and personally mentoring faculty through the instructional design workshops.

Design Process & Strategies

I used a train-the-trainer / capacity-building model: rather than just producing content for the university, I ran instructional design workshops grounded in ADDIE so faculty could analyze their own course gaps, design objectives, and build accessible multimedia content independently going forward. The LMS overhaul itself prioritized accessibility-first integration of learning assets, with pilot testing and stakeholder workshops feeding into a continuous improvement plan rather than a one-time handover.

The Solution

A modernized Moodle virtual campus plus a faculty training program and Rise-built sample module demonstrating the new content standard.
