ILO–KSTVET Partnership (PROSPECTS Project)

International Labour Organization & Kenya School of TVET · 2023 · Storyline / Elucidat

Sample 1 Sample 2 Sample 3

The Problem

Limited digitized content existed for trade courses at Kenya School of TVET, constraining the ILO-KSTVET PROSPECTS Project's ability to deliver competency-based vocational training at scale across multiple trades.

Target Audience

TVET trainees across nine trade courses — including electrical installation, welding, and entrepreneurship — with varying literacy and hands-on skill levels, plus shared common-unit learners across trades.

My Role

Instructional designer leading the digitization of trade course content, working with multimedia developers, a QA team, trainers, and support engineers through pilot rollout and stakeholder workshops.

Design Process & Strategies

Each trade course was designed around competency-based instructional design — mapping content directly to the practical skills trainees needed to demonstrate, rather than generic theory. I used task-based interactive modules that simulate real trade tasks (e.g. walking through an electrical installation procedure step-by-step) so the digital format mirrors hands-on workshop learning. Common units were developed once and reused across the nine trade courses for consistency, all packaged to SCORM for LMS delivery, with QA checks and a pilot rollout before full release and continuous feedback integration.

The Solution

Nine digitized, competency-based trade courses plus shared common units, built in Storyline and Elucidat and packaged to SCORM for delivery on KSTVET's LMS.
