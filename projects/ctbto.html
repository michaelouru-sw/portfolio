Enterprise LMS Deployment

CTBTO · 2023 · Moodle & Learning Passport on AWS / Azure

View Learning Management System

The Problem

CTBTO needed a high-performance, secure, and accessible LMS that could serve 10,000+ users across distributed regional offices, replacing fragmented training delivery with a single cloud-based platform that could meet an intergovernmental body's strict security and procurement standards.

Target Audience

10,000+ international technical staff and personnel across CTBTO's global monitoring network, requiring secure, high-availability access from multiple regions and varying levels of organizational IT infrastructure.

My Role

LMS architect and project manager, leading cloud architects, LMS administrators, integration specialists, and QA engineers through an agile delivery process, while managing vendor coordination and stakeholder reporting for a process-driven, security-conscious client.

Design Process & Strategies

I ran delivery in agile sprints to accommodate CTBTO's distributed teams and evolving requirements, architecting Moodle and Learning Passport on AWS and Azure for redundancy and uptime. SCORM/xAPI compliance was built into the architecture from day one so all training data would be trackable and reportable. Given the security-sensitive nature of the client, I incorporated formal LMS security incident-handling procedures and a structured migration plan to move existing training content without disrupting active users.

The Solution

A scalable, cloud-hosted Moodle & Learning Passport LMS on AWS/Azure, supporting 10,000+ users with SCORM/xAPI integration and formal security incident handling.
