Nexera Learning Platform

Alanta Consulting · 2026 · Moodle LMS / Coaching

Visit Nexera Learning

The Problem

Alanta Consulting needed a dedicated digital learning environment to deliver their coaching programmes at scale - one that could house structured, multi-module content, support a coaching-first learner journey, and carry their brand identity. An off-the-shelf platform would not offer the flexibility required; a bespoke LMS built around their methodology would.

Target Audience

Professionals and organisations engaging with Alanta Consulting's coaching services - accessing structured coaching content, frameworks, and learning journeys through a dedicated platform at their own pace.

My Role

End-to-end LMS architect and instructional designer - from platform selection and server setup through to the design, build, and launch of all 7 coaching modules, branded theming, and go-live configuration.

Design Process & Approach

The design process began with Alanta's coaching methodology - understanding how their programmes are structured, what a learner's journey looks like, and what completion and progress signals matter to their coaches. From that, I mapped the 7-module architecture, defined the content hierarchy within each module, and chose Moodle as the platform for its flexibility in structuring learning pathways and its ability to be fully white-labelled under the Nexera brand. Theme customisation, enrolment flows, and completion tracking were all configured to match how Alanta's coaching relationships actually work - not generic LMS defaults.

The Solution

A fully deployed, branded learning management system live at nexera.alantaconsulting.com, built on Moodle and structured around Alanta's coaching framework:

  • 7 bespoke coaching modules - each designed around a distinct phase or competency in Alanta's coaching methodology, with structured activities, resources, and progression logic.
  • Coaching-first learning architecture - module sequencing, completion criteria, and learner dashboards designed to mirror how coaching relationships develop over time, not a generic course catalogue.
  • Full Moodle deployment - server provisioning, Moodle installation, plugin configuration, security hardening, and performance optimisation for production use.
  • Nexera brand identity - custom theme, colour palette, typography, and interface elements aligned with Alanta Consulting's brand, creating a seamless experience distinct from a generic Moodle install.
  • Enrolment and access management - configured enrolment flows, user roles, and cohort management to support Alanta's client onboarding process.
