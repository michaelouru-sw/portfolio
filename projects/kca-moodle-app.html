Moodle Mobile App — Custom Branding

KCA University · 2026 · Moodle Mobile / UI Branding

View Virtual Campus

The Problem

KCA University students accessing Moodle on mobile were using the generic Moodle app — a white-label product with no connection to the university's visual identity. The experience felt disconnected from the KCAU brand, reducing trust and engagement. The university needed its own app: one that students would recognise, download with confidence, and associate with their institution — not a third-party tool.

Target Audience

KCA University students accessing their courses, assignments, and learning materials on mobile devices — expecting a seamless, branded university experience consistent with other institutional touchpoints.

My Role

Led the end-to-end Moodle Mobile app customisation — from brand asset preparation and configuration through to deployment — covering app icon, splash screen, login screen, theme colours, and dashboard layout, all aligned to KCA University's visual identity.

Design Process & Approach

The work began with KCA University's existing brand guidelines — extracting the official colour palette, logo assets, and typography to ensure every customised element would feel native to the institution rather than grafted on. Each screen was configured with the university's brand in mind: the login screen as the first impression, the dashboard as the daily environment, and the app icon as the persistent identity on a student's home screen. The goal was a coherent branded experience across every touchpoint — not isolated customisation of individual screens.

The Solution

A fully white-labelled KCAU Moodle Mobile app with end-to-end brand customisation across all key surfaces:

  • Custom app icon — KCAU-branded icon for iOS and Android home screens, replacing the generic Moodle logo with the university's own identity.
  • Splash screen — branded loading screen with the university logo and colour scheme, setting the tone from the moment the app opens.
  • Login screen — custom layout with KCAU branding, institution name, and colour palette — the first authenticated experience for every student.
  • Theme colours — university colour palette applied consistently across navigation bars, buttons, active states, and UI accents throughout the app.
  • Dashboard layout — configured to surface the most relevant course and activity information for KCAU students in a clean, branded environment.
  • Mobile app branding — full Moodle Mobile customisation configuration ensuring a coherent KCAU experience on both iOS and Android.

Results & Impact

Students access learning on KCAU's own branded app
Consistent brand across web & mobile
iOS & Android deployment
Improved student trust & recognition

Technical Competencies Demonstrated

  • Moodle Mobile app customisation (Moodle App Customisation Layer)
  • Brand identity translation to UI — colours, typography, iconography
  • App icon and splash screen asset production for iOS and Android
  • Moodle site configuration for mobile app integration
  • Cross-platform mobile UX consistency
