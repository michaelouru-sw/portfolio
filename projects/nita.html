Digital Content Development

National Industrial Training Authority (NITA), Kenya · 2023 · Storyline / Video Production

View NITA LMS Sample 1 Sample 2 Sample 3

The Problem

NITA regulates industrial training across Kenya but faced a shortage of well-curated digital courses to populate its LMS, leaving a gap between its regulatory mandate for quality workforce development and the content actually available to trainees.

Target Audience

Kenyan industrial workforce trainees with varying technical backgrounds, accessing structured, hands-on skills content through NITA's LMS.

My Role

Instructional designer and video producer, running needs analysis and SME consultations and coordinating with multimedia developers, QA testers, and LMS administrators through pilot rollout and evaluation.

Design Process & Strategies

I followed an ADDIE-based workflow: needs analysis with SMEs, an instructional design plan, and a content storyboard before production. Because the content was industrial/technical training, I leaned on video-based demonstration learning — showing real procedures and equipment rather than abstract slides — to better match how trainees actually learn hands-on trade skills. Each video was packaged into SCORM-compliant modules for LMS tracking, followed by a pilot test and QA pass before upload and post-launch evaluation.

The Solution

SCORM-compliant video-based training modules tailored to NITA's industrial training mandate, deployed on its national LMS.
