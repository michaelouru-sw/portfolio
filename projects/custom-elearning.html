The Problem

NGOs, corporates, and government entities across East Africa, Europe, and North America each had distinct performance gaps — from security-awareness training to LMS adoption — but lacked the in-house instructional design or LMS expertise to address them. There was no single off-the-shelf solution that fit such varied sectors and regions.

Target Audience

Corporate employees, NGO field staff, and government trainees spanning a wide range of language, literacy, and technology-access contexts across three continents.

My Role

Founder and lead instructional designer at Instracta, managing end-to-end delivery — instructional design, LMS consulting, custom content development, and train-the-trainer programs — with a flexible team of LMS consultants, multimedia developers, localization experts, and QA specialists per engagement.

Design Process & Strategies

Because client needs varied so widely, I matched the methodology to the engagement: a hybrid ADDIE/SAM approach for fast-turnaround corporate projects, and fuller needs-analysis cycles for government and NGO clients with compliance requirements. For security-awareness and behavior-change training, I used scenario-based learning — for example a realistic phishing-email simulation that lets learners practice spotting threats in context rather than memorizing rules. Train-the-trainer models were used where clients needed internal capacity rather than one-off content. Localization and translation were built into the workflow from the outset for multi-region delivery.

The Solution

A portfolio of custom SCORM/xAPI-compliant modules and LMS consulting engagements, including a scenario-based phishing-awareness simulation and a sample training LMS deployment.
