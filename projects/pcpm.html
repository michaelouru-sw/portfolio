PCPM eLearning Support

Polish Center for International Aid (PCPM) · 2023 · Moodle

The Problem

PCPM needed technical and instructional support to manage digital training content and learner engagement for firefighting training — a time-sensitive operational training need with no dedicated LMS support staff in-house.

Target Audience

Firefighting trainees and field staff with varying digital literacy, needing structured onboarding into a new LMS environment alongside their operational training.

My Role

LMS administrator and instructional support lead, handling setup, content and assessment upload, and learner onboarding, working alongside trainers, QA staff, and support engineers.

Design Process & Strategies

This was a rapid operational-support engagement rather than a long-cycle design project: configuring the LMS, uploading course units, assessments, and assignments, and running structured trainee orientation sessions to onboard learners quickly and maximize engagement from day one. QA checks and stakeholder workshops ran in parallel to catch issues before they affected active trainees, with a monitoring framework to support PCPM after handover.

The Solution

A fully configured Moodle LMS with uploaded course units, assessments, and assignments, plus structured learner onboarding for firefighting trainees.

Internal training platform — not publicly linkable.

Results & Impact

Effective firefighting training delivery
Improved learner engagement

  • Learner onboarding & orientation design
