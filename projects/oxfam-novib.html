Digital Influencing eLearning Module

Oxfam Novib · 2024 · Built in Elucidat

View on DisasterReady.org Launch Sample Module

The Problem

Oxfam Novib relied on live webinars to train partner organizations and field staff in digital influencing and advocacy techniques. Webinars were effective in the moment but couldn't scale: sessions were limited in reach, offered little interactivity, weren't recorded in a structured trackable format, and excluded audiences with unreliable internet access. Oxfam needed a self-paced, SCORM-compliant module that preserved the substance of the live training while making it globally accessible and measurable.

Target Audience

NGO program officers, advocacy coordinators, and partner-organization staff across Oxfam Novib's global network - adult professional learners with varying digital literacy, often connecting from low-bandwidth environments and fitting learning around full-time field work.

My Role

Lead instructional designer and PMP-certified project manager. I ran the needs assessment, owned the instructional design blueprint, and coordinated a cross-functional team of SMEs, a multimedia specialist, an accessibility expert, and QA/localization testers through to deployment on DisasterReady.org.

Design Process & Strategies

I followed an ADDIE workflow: a needs assessment and SME interviews to extract the core content from the live webinars, followed by a content storyboard and instructional design blueprint mapping objectives to interactions. Rather than a passive video re-recording, I converted key sections into scenario-based learning - learners work through realistic advocacy decision points and get branching feedback - to keep the self-paced format as engaging as the live sessions. The module was built in Elucidat for rapid SME review cycles, packaged to SCORM for tracking, and localized for cultural and linguistic relevance before a pilot test and iterative revision cycle.

The Solution

A SCORM-compliant, scenario-based Elucidat module deployed on DisasterReady.org, with interactive quizzes, branching scenarios, and full accessibility compliance baked in.
