AfCFTA Market Access eLearning Modules

African Continental Free Trade Area · March 2026 · Articulate Rise / Vyond

Watch Sample Video

The Brief

Request for Expressions of Interest: Consulting Services to Develop Simplified Industry Guides, E-Learning Modules, and Facilitate Training on Market Access under the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) for the Textile, Leather, and Edible Oil Value Chains. Posted March 24, 2026. The goal was to translate complex AfCFTA trade policy into practical, accessible eLearning for practitioners across the three value chains.

Target Audience

Business owners, trade practitioners, SMEs, and industry associations across the African continent operating in the Textile, Leather, and Edible Oil value chains - needing practical, accessible guidance on how to leverage AfCFTA market access rules to grow cross-border trade.

My Role

eLearning Module Developer - responsible for translating complex AfCFTA trade policy and market access content into structured, multimedia eLearning modules. Worked collaboratively with professors and subject-matter experts in international trade policy, value chain analysis, and continental market dynamics.

Design Process

The central challenge was simplification without distortion: AfCFTA's rules of origin, tariff schedules, and market access protocols are technically complex, but the audience is practitioners, not policy lawyers. I worked closely with trade professors and continental experts to extract the operationally relevant content from each value chain, then applied an instructional design layer to structure it as a learner journey. Each module moves from context (why AfCFTA matters for this value chain) through mechanics (how to access and apply the relevant provisions) to application (practical steps and worked examples). Video was used to humanise the policy content, and each module is packaged for standalone delivery.

The Solution

A suite of eLearning modules covering market access under AfCFTA across three priority value chains:

  • Textile value chain - rules of origin, preferential tariffs, and cross-border trade mechanics specific to fabric and garment production.
  • Leather value chain - market access provisions, certification requirements, and trade facilitation opportunities for leather goods manufacturers.
  • Edible Oil value chain - tariff schedule navigation, sanitary and phytosanitary provisions, and practical export readiness guidance.
  • Simplified industry guides - companion reference documents distilling module content into quick-reference tools for practitioners in the field.
  • Video-based instruction - produced with Vyond to explain policy concepts accessibly, using animated scenarios grounded in real trade situations.
  • Expert collaboration - content validated and co-developed with professors and specialists in AfCFTA, international trade law, and the three value chains.
