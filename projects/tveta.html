TVETA eLearning Standards Development

TVETA, Kenya · 2024 · Standards & Policy

View Related LMS

The Problem

TVET institutions across Kenya had no standardized framework for eLearning quality assurance and accessibility, leading to inconsistent learner experiences from one institution to the next. TVETA needed a national standard that any TVET institution could adopt.

Target Audience

TVET institution administrators, instructors, and curriculum developers across Kenya who would implement the standards, and ultimately the TVET trainees who benefit from more consistent, accessible eLearning.

My Role

Contributing instructional design and accessibility specialist on the policy-development working group, alongside policy experts, QA staff, trainers, and stakeholder coordinators.

Design Process & Strategies

This was a standards-development process rather than a single course build: drafting a standards framework and accessibility guidelines, running stakeholder workshops to pressure-test them against real institutional constraints, pilot testing the draft standards in select institutions, and iterating through feedback loops referencing established accessibility frameworks (WCAG-aligned) before national publication and training sessions to support adoption.

The Solution

A national standards framework, accessibility guidelines, and QA tools for eLearning across TVET institutions, with training and monitoring to support adoption.
